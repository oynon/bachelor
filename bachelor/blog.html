8/26/26: Night Shift At The Library I'm back from the mountains, which means I'm also back to my normal, everyday life. Coincidentally, I started a new job just a few days after getting back, so I've really been digging my teeth into that and trying to learn the ins and outs of everything. As you can probably guess from the title of this blog post, I'm working at a library, and there's honestly something really weird and unexplainable about working the night shift at the library. Since nobody really goes to the library at night anyway, it's always just me there, which gives me a lot of time to work on some really productive, enriching activities... you know, like reading the Haruhi Suzumiya books on the clock and only half-paying attention to my homework. It's a really riveting affair (lol) but in all seriousness I do genuinely like the place and the job. Honestly, it's the type of job I've wanted to have (and the type of place I've wanted to work at) for a really long time now, and as quiet as things are during my night shift, I really am getting a lot done. Mostly in the way of watching an insane amount of anime during my shift. This is, of course, to say that if you have any recommendations for things I can read/watch/play on the clock I will happily take them. I've been making some really big gains on my Japanese listening ability lately (mostly owing to me watching like every single available episode of Sayonara Lara lately... how come nobody told me it was that good?), which has been really pleasant, and I added some more great resources to my little section on the sidebar :P hopefully somebody gets some use out of those. I'm happy to be back and working on Bachelor again.
