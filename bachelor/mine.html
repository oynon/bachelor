Things I made, last updated 11/9/26
I made this page mostly to call attention to HAVE MERCY ON ME, my debut visual novel. It's about 7 hours, if I had to guess. Detailed content warnings are available in the README.md file and the game itself; off the top of my head, it contains strong language, potentially startling noises, references to the apocalypse, graphic depictions of violence (written), references to substance abuse and a few other things. There are two routes. Let me know if you need help finding either of them.
Synopsis:
On October 24th, 〇〇〇〇, a young man leaves his apartment intending to drown himself in the river. His plan is interrupted when a space shuttle crashes to Earth; its sole survivor, an alien surveyor called Kyrie Eleison, comes bearing news. The world will end in a week. The clock cannot be stopped. There is only ‘forward’..

Forced to keep Kyrie in his apartment for their safety, he finds himself disillusioned and unsure of how to spend the remainder of his time. Kyrie’s proposal: “let’s make a song to close out ‘humanity’, something so powerful and pure that even the stars will weep”.

HAVE MERCY ON ME is a visual novel featuring music-composition gameplay and roughly 7 hours of playtime. There are two routes and a diverse range of dialogue choices; the songs you make cannot alter the fate of the world, but they might change yours. Bide your time. Build effigies. Dive in.
My game is available on a pay-what-you-want basis, which is a fancy way of saying you can get it for free :] making it took a lot of time and effort, so if you're in the mood to buy or post about it, I'd build a sculpture in your likeness.
